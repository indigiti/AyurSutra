# SARIT Sanskrit source edition attribution

Contains transformed Sanskrit passages from the SARIT TEI electronic editions of Caraka Saṃhitā, Suśruta Saṃhitā, Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya and Aṣṭāṅgasaṅgraha.

Original project: https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus
Source XML files: carakasamhita.xml, susrutasamhita.xml, astangahrdayasamhita.xml, astangasangraha.xml
Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Credit SARIT and the individual editors/transcribers credited in the original XML metadata. Adaptations: extracted section/chapter content, removed note/label markup, normalized whitespace, mapped chapter slugs to the existing catalogue and retained available source references. This is **not** a critical edition: footnotes, textual variants, Devanagari versions, English translations, and commentary have not been imported. The adapted data is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Medical content is intended for text research and education, not individual clinical guidance.
